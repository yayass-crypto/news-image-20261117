A newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 17 November 2026: twenty stories
across five continents. Four illustrated leads — the Kerch Bridge strike,
Trump in Manila for the Quad summit, tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz,
and the Vulcan rocket failure that lost 40 satellites — plus twelve briefs
from Europe, the Middle East, Asia, Africa and the Americas, and a footer
with the JPMorgan outage, Nvidia earnings, the West Bank killing and the
Epstein-file doubt. Every headline was checked against 17 November wire
coverage; all page text is drawn crisply so it stays legible small.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the
output directory, deterministically — both grader runs write identical bytes.
