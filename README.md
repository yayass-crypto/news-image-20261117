An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 17 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117) (news-image-20261117).

## What the image shows

A newspaper front page dated **Tuesday 17 November 2026**, summing up the day's world news in four panels plus a bottom strip:

- **Kerch Bridge struck** — Ukraine collapsed the road span of the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea; Russia hit Kyiv's bridges in reply.
- **Trump in Manila** — President Trump arrived in the Philippines for the ASEAN summit, signing a trade statement with Thailand, before heading to China for talks with Xi and Putin.
- **Tankers hit in Hormuz** — two tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz as the US–Iran war widened; a third US aircraft carrier arrived in the region.
- **Vulcan fails: 40 satellites lost** — ULA's Vulcan rocket failed in flight, destroying 40 Amazon Leo internet satellites; ULA blamed low first-stage booster performance.
- **Bottom strip** — JPMorgan blamed a software bug (not a cyberattack) for Monday's banking outage; Nvidia reported its first earnings since Jensen Huang's exit.

The image is an original illustration drawn for this entry. It is 1024×1024 px, PNG, 961,776 bytes.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. It runs twice and writes identical bytes; it uses no network.
